About the job

Salesforce AI Developer


Experience: 10+ years overall, including 2+ years of hands-on Salesforce AI development

Location: US Remote


Location & Background Check Requirements

Candidate must currently reside in one of the following states:

California, Texas, Massachusetts, Illinois, New York, Colorado, Connecticut, or New Jersey.

A strict background check is mandatory. Failure to successfully clear the required background screening will make the candidate ineligible for this contract opportunity.

Job Summary


We are seeking a senior Salesforce AI Developer with strong Salesforce engineering experience and hands-on expertise building AI and agentic solutions using Agentforce, AIforce/SIforce, Headless 360, MCP, Apex, Flow, LWC, and Salesforce APIs.

The role will focus on building secure, scalable Salesforce AI capabilities, headless integrations, and agent-driven workflows across enterprise business processes.

Key Responsibilities


  • Build AI and agentic solutions using Agentforce, AIforce/SIforce, Headless 360, and Generative AI.
  • Develop and integrate Salesforce Hosted MCP Servers, MCP tools, Apex services, Flows, and APIs.
  • Enable secure headless access to Salesforce data and business actions for AI agents and external platforms.
  • Build Agentforce agents, topics, actions, prompts, and orchestration workflows.
  • Develop Salesforce solutions using Apex, LWC, Flow, SOQL, Platform Events, and REST APIs.
  • Integrate Salesforce with LLMs, middleware, enterprise applications, and external APIs.
  • Implement OAuth, permissions, sharing, API security, grounding, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop controls.
  • Support testing, troubleshooting, deployment, observability, and production readiness.


Required Qualifications


  • 10+ years of overall IT/Salesforce experience.
  • 2+ years of hands-on Salesforce AI or Generative AI development experience.
  • Strong experience with Agentforce, AIforce/SIforce, Headless 360, and Salesforce Hosted MCP Servers.
  • Hands-on experience developing or consuming MCP servers and tools.
  • Strong development experience with Apex, LWC, Flow, SOQL, Salesforce APIs, and asynchronous processing.
  • Experience with OAuth, Connected/External Client Apps, Salesforce security, permissions, and sharing models.
  • Strong understanding of LLMs, RAG, prompt engineering, grounding, tool calling, and agentic workflows.
  • Experience integrating Salesforce with enterprise systems, middleware, and AI platforms.



Preferred Qualifications


  • Experience building custom MCP tools using Apex and Flow.
  • Experience with Salesforce Data Cloud/Data 360, MuleSoft, or event-driven architectures.
  • Experience building autonomous or semi-autonomous workflows involving recommendations, approvals, and next-best actions.
  • Relevant Salesforce certifications such as Platform Developer I/II, Application Architect, System Architect, or Agentforce/AI certifications.
